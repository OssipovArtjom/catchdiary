Catch: Support

Catch is a private fishing diary for iPhone. If something does not work, or you have a question about your data, write to us.

Email: support@catchdiary.app

Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?
Open the Profile tab, tap the gear icon, then Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, diary and photos.

Why is my catch marked "Waiting for network"?
Catch saves a catch on your phone first, then uploads it. It will upload by itself when you have a connection.

Where is my data stored?
In your private account in the cloud, with a copy on your phone. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
