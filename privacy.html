Privacy Policy for Catch

Effective date: 19 November 2026
App: Catch: Fishing Diary (iOS)
Developer: Artjom Ossipov
Contact: support@catchdiary.app

Catch is a personal fishing diary. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language. The short version: your diary is private, we do not show ads, we do not track you, and we do not sell your data.

1. Information we handle

Account

  • Your email address and password. The password is stored only in hashed form by our backend provider; we cannot read it. If you ask to reset your password, we use the email address to send you a one-time code.
  • A sign-in session, kept in the iOS Keychain on your device so you stay signed in.

Profile

  • A username you choose (required), and an optional profile photo and short bio.

Your fishing diary

  • For each catch: species, weight, length, bait, date and time, your notes, and up to three photos.
  • An optional location for the catch (a point on the map). You can set it by tapping the map, from your current position, or from the location stored in a photo.
  • Whether you marked the catch as a favourite.
  • The weather at the time of the catch (temperature, sky, wind, pressure), looked up for you (see section 3).

On your device only (never sent to us)

  • Your settings (metric or imperial units, list view style) and your favourites in the Guide.
  • A local copy of your diary and photos so the app opens without a connection, and catches waiting to upload when the network returns.

We do not collect your contacts, your photo library as a whole, your advertising identifier, your device identifiers, or any analytics or crash data.

2. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera: only to take a photo of a catch when you tap "Take photo".
  • Location (while using the app): only to show where you are on the map and to suggest a place for a new catch. Catch does not track your location in the background.
  • Photos: you choose photos with the system picker; the app sees only the photos you select. If a selected photo has a date or location stored in it, the app reads that on your device to fill in the form. The copy that is uploaded has this hidden metadata removed.

You can change or withdraw each permission at any time in iOS Settings. The app still works without them; you just enter the information by hand.

3. Who else receives data

We use the following services to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only as needed.

  • Supabase (supabase.com): hosting, database, file storage and sign-in. It stores your account, profile, diary entries and photos. Our database is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Your diary and photos are in private storage that only your signed-in account can read.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): a free weather service. When you save a catch that has a location, the app sends the date and the location rounded to about 1 km to look up the weather. Nothing else is sent. Like any internet service, Open-Meteo also sees your device's IP address.
  • Resend (resend.com): an email delivery service. When you ask to reset your password, it delivers the one-time code to your email address. It receives your email address and the message only for that purpose.
  • Apple: the maps in the app are Apple Maps. When you ask for directions, the app hands the catch's coordinates to the navigation app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze), which is then governed by that app's own policy. Apple also handles the App Store and any crash or usage data you choose to share with developers through iOS settings.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics software.

4. Sharing a catch

Catch can create a picture of a catch (your chosen photo, species, weight and length). It is created on your device. Nothing is uploaded to share it: the picture goes only where you send it with the iOS share sheet. Once you send it, it is outside our control.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the diary: save, show, edit and delete your catches.
  • To keep your account working and secure.
  • To add weather and moon information to your catches and to show your statistics.

We do not use your content for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

6. Who can see your diary

Only you. Other users of the app cannot see it. The app owner can technically access the database in order to run and maintain the service, but we do not look at your content except where needed to operate the service, fix a problem you report, or meet a legal obligation.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it. You can delete a single catch at any time. You can delete your whole account in Settings → Delete account: this permanently removes your profile, diary entries and photos from our servers and clears the copy stored on your device. Copies held in the provider's routine backups, if any, are overwritten on the provider's normal schedule.

If you delete the app without deleting your account, your data stays on our servers until you sign back in and delete it, or contact us to have it deleted.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. Photos are in private storage, and database access is restricted so that each account can reach only its own rows. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or take a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict some processing, and to withdraw consent. You can already edit and delete most of your data inside the app. For anything else, write to support@catchdiary.app and we will answer within a reasonable time (within one month where the law requires). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Our legal basis for handling your data is that it is necessary to provide the service you asked for (your diary), and, for optional permissions such as location and camera, your consent.

10. Children

Catch is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the local law sets a higher age) and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where the law requires it, they rely on recognised safeguards. See the providers' own privacy policies for details.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for meaningful changes we will tell you in the app. The latest version is always available in the app under Settings → Privacy policy and at the web address given on our App Store page.
